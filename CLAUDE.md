# Night Shift — read before every change

A talk-only story game: the Look Busy office after hours (split off from
`humble-house-studios/Look-busy`), one night from 11:58 PM to 6:00 AM in
three acts up the corporate ladder: ACT ONE the floor you work on (Wendy,
Kip, Printy, Dale), ACT TWO middle management (Marge walking her loop, Chad
frozen on a video call, Brenda in HR; the 3:33 blackout), ACT THREE the top
(the board, the empty-suit CEO, Dale the founder). Say no at the top and the
elevator finds ROOF: a wheat field under an impressionist sky, everyone you
got out standing in it, a dream-pop trance track, the credits rolling.
Godot 4.3, GDScript only. Story design: docs/story.md.

Samuel builds from his phone: Claude writes and pushes the code, GitHub builds a
web version, and he playtests it in Safari. Keep replies short and in plain
words, and handle git, builds and fixes yourself.
**End every reply with the play link:** https://humble-house-studios.github.io/Night-shift-/

## Same rules as Look Busy
- Base 720×1280, `canvas_items` + `expand`; sideways swaps to 1280×720
  (`main.gd` `_layout()`), phones lock portrait, desktop opens 1280×720.
- Compatibility renderer, nothing web-only (wrap in `OS.has_feature`), touch
  and mouse both work, buttons at least 88 px tall, respect the safe area,
  web build light (no threads).
- **Everything is hand-made**: build models from `addons/hh_minigames/_shared/handmade.gd`
  through `_box` / `_cyl` / `_lump` / `_ball` in `scenes/world/night_office.gd`
  (copied from Look Busy's office.gd so the two games look like one).
  People are puppets (`scenes/world/puppet.gd`, Look Busy's). No image generation.
- Sounds: Look Busy's in `audio/`, plus Night Shift's own from
  `tools/make_audio.py` (blip, print, thump, night drone).

## Voices
Every line is spoken, recorded with **Kokoro** (free open-source neural
voice, `tools/record_voices.py`) on GitHub's build machine: no API keys, no
daily limits, no credits. Cast and studio treatment per character in `CAST`
there (Night Manager through a phone, Printy robotic). The deploy's `voices`
job records only new or changed lines (folder cached), the build plays
`audio/voice/<md5("who|text")[:12]>.ogg` per page (`main.gd` `_clip()`), and
the text types along with the voice. **After editing the script run
`godot --headless -s res://tools/export_lines.gd`** (writes
`data/voice_lines.json`; `tests/test_voices.gd` fails if you forget).
Bump `VERSION` in record_voices.py to re-record everything after recasting.
**Dale** speaks in his Look Busy voice (Groq Orpheus "troy", Samuel's pick)
through Look Busy's voice server (`VOICE_URL` in deploy.yml); Groq's free
allowance is small, so up to 30 of his lines a run, Kokoro standing in until
each has its take (`audio/voice/engines.json`).

## Score and sounds
- Ambient score: Creative Commons tracks fetched in CI by `tools/fetch_score.py`
  (cues `title`, `night` before 3:33, `late` after; first track per cue that
  downloads wins, faded into a loop), credited in CREDITS from
  `audio/score/credits.json`. Missing = the game's own drone (title: Look
  Busy's "Clock-In Ballad"). `main.gd` `_cue()` crossfades.
- The field and credits: cue `field`, a Creative Commons dreamy synthwave
  track ("Dreamstate", Bogart VGM, CC BY 3.0) played once, not looped; the
  original `audio/heaven.ogg` (`tools/make_heaven.py`) is the fallback. On the
  way to the field everything else fades out first, then a warm white-out.
- SFX: soft, glassy, a little psychedelic, generated by `tools/make_audio.py`
  (choice pluck, shimmer for items, swell gliding to someone, tape stop at
  the blackout, wrong-note bells, page tick).

## Cards, relics, collection
`data/collectibles.gd`: nine GOAL CARDS (the three things each floor's
elevator wants) and ten RELICS (keepsakes for being kind), each keyed by the
story flag that earns it. `scenes/story/collect.gd` reveals each one as it's
earned (a card flips up, tap to keep), shows this floor's cards as chips and
the relics as icons under the clock, and keeps the COLLECTION across nights
(`user://collection.cfg`; pause menu or title). Cards are drawn in code
(`card.gd`, icons in `icons.gd`). `tests/test_dev.gd` checks the kind path
earns all of them.

## Dev mode
`engine/dev_jump.gd` (`DevJump.ON`; set false before shipping): DEV in the
pause menu and on the title jumps to any of twelve moments (wake, the 1:11
turn, each arrival, 3:33, Dale on the stairs, the offer, both endings) by
really playing the story up to it, then reloads into it. Also unlock/wipe the
collection. Add a checkpoint when you add a big moment.

## Title sequence
`scenes/story/title.gd`, Xbox 360 style, filmed live in the office: close on
the "Golden Hour" painting (front wall) as its sun sinks, Dale against the
window, a crane over the office as night falls and the CRTs come on, then
the sunset-gold logo (`night_logo.gdshader`), PRESS START and a menu with a
sliding glossy bar. Bloom/grade/grain via `title_post.gdshader` on the
world view. The first tap only turns the sound on; the next skips to the logo. `TITLE_ONLY=1` with tools/shot.gd shoots it.

## Where things are
- `data/night_script.gd`: the whole story. Nodes (who, pages, choices, flags,
  clock, events), who you can tap (`SPOTS`), which conversation each thing
  starts (`ENTRIES`, first condition that holds), conversations that start by
  themselves (`AUTO`: prologue, the 3:33 blackout), the rules card, the
  epilogue lines. Pages are `"text"` or `"speaker|text"`.
- `engine/story.gd` (`NightStory`): runs the script, no scene code. Flags,
  clock, items, save (`user://night_shift.cfg`).
- `scenes/world/night_office.gd`: builds every floor, the people and props.
  Floor one at the origin (the office's fixed lights in ps1.gdshader, plus
  `set_night` / `set_lights` / `set_slot`); floors two and three and the field
  far off along x (`ORIGIN`), each with its own six lights, ambient and haze
  (`_begin_room` / `room_light` / `room_ambient`: their materials set
  `office_lights` 0). Marge's walk (`ROUTE`, `marge_at`), Chad's TV diorama,
  the CEO chair, the field (`scenes/field/` sky, wheat, ground shaders; wheat
  is one MultiMesh; `field_cast()` stands whoever you saved in it).
- `data/night_script.gd` also holds `GOALS` (three per act), `SAVED` (seven
  people), `EPILOGUE` / `LEFT` / `NAMES` for the credits, and `SPOTS` with a
  floor each. `story.act()` = the floor you're on.
- `scenes/story/main.gd`: camera, walking, tapping, the talk box, HUD, events
  (`_event()`: ring, blackout, print, dawn...), title and ending screens.

## Testing
- `godot --headless -s addons/gut/gut_cmdln.gd`: every link in the script
  exists and whole nights play to each ending (`tests/test_story.gd`). Add a
  test path when you add a branch.
- `xvfb-run -a godot --rendering-driver opengl3 -s res://tools/shot.gd`
  plays the whole night through the real UI, saves screenshots (`OUT=`,
  `SIZE=720x1280` for upright) and prints PASS/FAIL.
- After each change: push to `main`, wait for the build, send Samuel the play
  link with one line on what changed and what to playtest.
