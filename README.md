# Night Shift

A talk-only story game split off from [Look Busy](https://github.com/humble-house-studios/Look-busy):
the same PS1 office, after hours. You fell asleep at your desk on your first
day at Humble House Games. It's 11:58 PM, every monitor is still on, and the
Night Manager has extended your shift. To clock out you need your timecard, a
manager's signature and the override code, and each one is held by someone
who has been stuck here a lot longer than you.

No mini games, no fail states: walk the office, talk, choose what to say.
One night, about 20 minutes, two endings (and a loop).

**Play the latest build:** https://humble-house-studios.github.io/Night-shift-/

Phone upright = mobile layout, sideways = Steam layout. Tap someone to talk,
tap the floor to walk, drag to look. Keyboard (WASD, Space, 1-4) and an Xbox
pad work too.

Built in Godot 4.3. Every push to `main` runs the story tests and rebuilds the
link (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). Project rules are in `CLAUDE.md`, the
story design in `docs/story.md`.
